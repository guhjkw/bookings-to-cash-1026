How it works

Your Bookeo, Xero and PayZen flow keeps running as it does today. This sits on top, checks it every morning, and fixes what slipped.

01 · YOUGive read accessBookeo, Xero and PayZen, plus the Make scenarios that already join them.
02Matched every morningEach booking lined up with its invoice and every payment on it, refunds included.
03Only exceptions shownSync failures, expired or refused links, part payments, double payments and cancellations with money attached.
04Fixed in one clickUpdates go back to Xero and Bookeo, and customers get their message in French or English.

Under the hood

The checker is one logic file. The page you just used and 66 automated checks both load that same file, so what the tests prove is what you clicked. Hand src/ and tests/ to your developer, or never open them.

In the paid build the same logic reads Bookeo, Xero and PayZen through their APIs each morning, writes the fixes back, and sends the messages from your address.
